Using my own laptop, on Fedora (32 hardware threads), `g++ -O2 -std=c++17 -fopenmp`,
n = 100,000 `rand()` values, 8 bits per digit, 5 runs per execution, 5 executions.
Times are the median of the 5 executions.
We see an improvement, but it's quite light. I'd say the openMP threads coordination is the culprit, + Amdham's law obviously. I'll now try to parallelize the scatter part as well.
