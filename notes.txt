 10/10:
 Today I tried to make the comparison more fair: I made all the different version void + reusing the ans vector and swapping it with vec at each iteration. For non parallel functions, I also changed std::max_element because when I ran radix_sort_byte with std::max_element vs radix_sort_byte_parallel on ONE THREAD, the parallel version was faster, which induces the fact that a basic for loop to find the max IS faster than max_element. So I replaced max_element by a basic for loop for the sequential and non parallel byte version. I would now say that the comparison are fair, and here are the result of the run:
